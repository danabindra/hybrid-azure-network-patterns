## Bow-Tie Model

The bowtie configuration between Central US and East US2 represents a simpler approach for regional redundancy. The focus is on maintaining continuity between paired Azure regions and leveraging direct interconnects for quick failover and synced recovery. Redundancy is limited to the two region relationship, ( broader outages outside those hubs require secondary recovery paths) to be manually activated through ExpressRoute or other corporate backbone rerouting. Needs applications clearly defined as primary and DR regions but does not provide the same level of distributed fault tolerance or TE flexibility
