The bowtie configuration between Central US and East US2 represents a simpler but more tightly coupled regional redundancy model. It primarily focuses on maintaining continuity between two paired Azure regions, leveraging direct interconnects for low-latency failover and synchronized recovery operations. While it’s more efficient to maintain, its redundancy is limited to that two-region relationship, meaning broader outages outside those hubs could require secondary recovery paths to be manually activated through ExpressRoute or backbone-level rerouting. For FedEx, this model may be suitable for applications that have clearly defined primary and DR regions but does not provide the same level of distributed fault tolerance or traffic engineering flexibility that a four-point mesh can offer.
